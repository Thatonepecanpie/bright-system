## What it is

A showcase of nine website designs, shown as a 3x3 grid of cards. Each card has a Figma badge, so these are
Figma files, probably templates or concepts the author sells or shares. Four cards carry a "New" badge. Each
card shows the top of the site (the hero), so this is a library of first impressions rather than full sites.

## The gallery frame itself

The grid is worth copying in its own right. It has a black background and rounded cards with even gaps of
about 16px. Under each card there's a caption row: the name on the left in a clean white sans serif, and the
category on the right in small grey **monospace** with spaced slashes ("AI / Tech", "E-commerce"). A
white-outlined "New" pill sits top-left and a round Figma badge bottom-left. It's simple, consistent and
looks expensive.

## The pieces

1. **Lumière (e-commerce, jewellery).** A warm cream page with a hairline nav. A pendant with a green oval stone
   hangs from a chain at the top centre, and eight product thumbnails circle it, alternating dark rounded
   squares and light circles. The headline sits left in a serif, "Jewellery for everyday rituals.", with a
   swash italic *J*. Small vertical labels sit on the right. Mostly empty space. *The idea: products orbit the
   hero piece.*
2. **Prisma (AI / tech).** Black with a red-orange glow rising from below. An exploded cube made of glossy red
   blocks fills the middle, each face marked with an outlined symbol (circle, cross, triangle, square,
   diamond). The headline is set in light all-caps top-left and ends "...the shape of what a cube can be";
   the first word was hidden by the badge. Giant pale numerals ("CNB" bottom-left, "27" bottom-right) run off
   the edges. *The idea: one 3D object repeated until it becomes texture, with type as a frame.*
3. **Nexus (AI / tech).** The page is split: the top part is a sunset gradient from red-orange to peach, the
   bottom is plain white, and a tiny face (two closed happy eyes and a smile) sits in the middle of the white.
   A cursor sits next to it, so the face may react to the pointer (uncertain). *The idea: a near-empty page
   with one character, so the AI feels friendly instead of techy.*
4. **Seed (a single hero section).** A full-bleed close-up of a dandelion in soft backlit green and gold, with
   a shallow focus and a vignette. Small, low-contrast UI text sits over it. *The idea: the mood is the
   message, carried by the photo.*
5. **Relay (SaaS).** Near-black, lit by a sweeping olive-green light streak. Standard nav, with a lime-green
   pill button top-right as the only bright colour. A short headline and supporting text sit in a right-hand
   column. This is the tile the post's video plays over. *The idea: a dark, cinematic SaaS page where the one
   acid accent marks the action.*
6. **AI Studio (AI / tech).** Violet-purple glow on dark. Big sans headline "Prompts that / think ahead". The
   second line is dimmer and cut off at "ahea", so it is probably being typed or revealed (uncertain). A
   moody sculptural portrait sits in a dark card overlapping the headline. A short paragraph with two small
   buttons (dark outline and light fill) sits lower-left. *The idea: the headline acts out what it says.*
7. **Artefakt (e-commerce, fashion).** Black. A glossy black puffer jacket with blue reflections fills the
   lower-left. Translucent violet 3D layers, like quilted padding, float at the top. "NEED TO KNOW." is set in
   a white pixel/monospace font, beside an FAQ laid out as a spec sheet: mono labels on the left, paragraphs on
   the right, hairline rules between rows. *The idea: an FAQ styled like a technical spec, so even the boring
   section has attitude.*
8. **Held-Mint (fintech, collectibles).** Dark navy. A heavy condensed uppercase headline is cropped at the top
   edge (possibly "CURRENTLY", uncertain). Five collectible character cards fan out in an arc, with the
   centre card ("Red Hype Ape") raised and frontmost. A pill-shaped strip of stats sits below, with a small
   blue crown icon and a white outlined button top-right. *The idea: a fan of cards says "collection" without
   any words.*
9. **Brewns (hospitality, coffee).** A light grey page. A thin clock face (numerals 1-12 and tick marks) with
   an iced latte (clear cup, black straw) standing at its centre as the hub. The nav at the top is split into
   bordered cells like a table. *The idea: a visual pun where the drink becomes the clock (coffee o'clock,
   or ordering by time), and nothing else competes with it.*

## Moves worth borrowing

- **One-idea hero.** Commit the first screen to a single image or pun (Brewns, Nexus, Prisma). For an Xglide
  landing page, find the product's own "coffee clock" before laying out anything else.
- **Orbit layout.** Arrange related items in a ring around the hero object (Lumière). This works for agents
  around a task, features around a product, or screens around a phone.
- **Fanned cards.** A slight arc with the centre card raised (Held-Mint). Good for showing several app
  screens, plans or team members at once.
- **Acid accent.** A dark or neutral page with exactly one bright colour, used only for the primary action
  (Relay, Prisma).
- **Headline that performs.** A second line that types itself in or fades up (AI Studio). Use it once per page
  at most.
- **Spec-sheet FAQ.** Mono labels, hairline rules and two columns (Artefakt). It turns dull content into a
  design element and fits dashboards and settings pages too.
- **Name + mono caption rows.** The gallery frame's caption style suits any grid of cards: Company Floor agent
  tiles, Xglide screens, a portfolio.
- **Giant cropped type.** Numerals or a headline so large they run off the edge and become a frame (Prisma,
  Held-Mint).

## Watch out for

- These are heroes only. They show nothing about forms, tables, navigation depth or empty states, so don't
  borrow their spectacle for working tools. Borrow the details instead.
- Most of them depend on custom 3D renders or art-directed photography. Without that imagery the layouts fall
  flat, so budget for it (see "Making it real" in SKILL.md).
- The low-contrast small text in Seed, Relay and Artefakt looks great in a thumbnail and is hard to read in
  real use. Keep body text at a readable contrast.
- I saw one frame of a video. If the video shows motion (the Nexus face, the AI Studio headline), ask Fernando
  for screenshots or a recording before relying on how it moves.
